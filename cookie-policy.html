How we use cookies and how to manage your preferences

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit our website. They allow websites to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences and settings.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality (login sessions for the admin dashboard, security tokens)
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences (e.g., menu state, mobile nav toggle)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use the website (via Supabase analytics) to improve our services
  • Consent Cookies: Remember your cookie and consent preferences

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
sb-consent Stores your cookie consent choice 12 months
session Admin dashboard login session Session (until logout)
_ga Google Analytics (if enabled) 2 years

4. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, we display a cookie consent banner. You can:

  • Accept All: Accept all cookies and consent is remembered
  • Reject: Reject non-essential cookies; we only set essential cookies
  • Manage Preferences: Choose which types of cookies you allow

Your choice is saved in localStorage and will be remembered on your next visit.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

In Your Browser: Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Other Site Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, Search, and Services → Cookies and Other Site Data

You can block all cookies or allow only essential cookies. Be aware that blocking cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Third-Party Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Supabase: Backend services (auth, database). See Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery. See Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps: Embedded maps on our contact page. See Google Privacy Policy

7. Disabling Cookies

You have the right to disable cookies. However, this may limit the functionality of our website, particularly:

  • Admin dashboard login may not work
  • Your cookie preferences may not be saved
  • Some interactive features may be limited

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised.
