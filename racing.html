❓ Question-Based Keywords
How do I prepare my vehicle for track day?

Track day preparation includes brake inspection, fluid checks, tire condition assessment, suspension checking, and safety equipment verification. Our technicians ensure your vehicle meets safety standards and performs optimally on circuit.

What is ECU tuning and how does it improve performance?

ECU remapping optimizes engine parameters for increased power and torque. Our professional tuning uses dyno testing to safely maximize performance while maintaining reliability and engine longevity.

How often should racing vehicles be serviced?

Race vehicles require more frequent servicing than road cars. We recommend pre-event inspections, mid-season services, and post-season maintenance depending on racing intensity and vehicle specifications.

Can you upgrade my braking system for performance?

Yes, we provide complete brake upgrades including high-performance pads, braided lines, and brake fluid servicing. Upgraded braking systems improve stopping power and reduce brake fade during competitive driving.

What suspension modifications improve handling?

Suspension upgrades include adjustable coilovers, upgraded anti-roll bars, and stiffer bushings. These modifications improve cornering response, reduce body roll, and optimize vehicle dynamics for racing conditions.

How much horsepower can I gain from tuning?

Power gains depend on your vehicle and tuning approach. ECU remapping typically increases 15-50+ hp depending on engine type. Combined with exhaust and intake modifications, gains can exceed 100 hp on some vehicles.

What is dyno testing and why is it important?

Dyno testing measures actual power output and allows safe tuning validation. It identifies issues, optimizes performance, and ensures modifications are effective and safe before racing.

Do you provide technical support during racing events?

Yes, we offer trackside support, pre-event briefings, and post-event analysis. Our team provides technical guidance to help you maximize performance and vehicle reliability during competition.
