❓ Common Questions & Featured Snippets
How often should I service my vehicle?

Most vehicles require servicing every 12 months or 10,000 miles. Regular servicing maintains manufacturer warranty, ensures reliability, and identifies potential issues before they become expensive repairs.

What is included in a car service?

A typical car service includes oil and filter changes, fluid checks, brake inspection, tire check, lighting system test, and engine diagnostics. Major services also include air filter, cabin filter, and spark plug replacement.

How much does car servicing cost?

Interim service costs £100-200, while major services range from £200-400. Costs vary by vehicle type, age, and local market. We provide transparent pricing with no hidden charges.

What does a warning light mean?

Warning lights indicate various issues from low oil pressure to engine faults. We use computer diagnostics to identify the exact problem and recommend appropriate repairs or maintenance.

How do I know if my brakes need repair?

Warning signs include brake noise, soft or spongy pedal, vibration during braking, or increased stopping distance. These indicate potential brake system issues requiring immediate inspection.

How long do brake pads last?

Brake pads typically last 25,000-70,000 miles depending on driving habits and conditions. We inspect brake pad thickness during servicing and recommend replacement before they damage rotors.

What is wheel alignment and why is it important?

Wheel alignment adjusts vehicle suspension angles for proper tire contact. Proper alignment improves handling, extends tire life, and ensures safe, efficient braking and steering.

When should I replace my battery?

Car batteries typically last 3-5 years. Cold weather, frequent short trips, and old age reduce battery life. We test battery health and recommend replacement before you're stranded with a dead battery.
