❓ Service Checklist Questions
What items are included in a van service checklist?

A comprehensive van service checklist includes oil change, filter replacement, fluid checks, brake inspection, tire assessment, lighting check, suspension inspection, exhaust examination, battery test, and safety feature testing.

How often should I service my van?

Most vans require servicing annually or every 10,000 miles. Some manufacturers recommend condition-based servicing based on oil condition monitoring. Check your owner's manual for specific intervals.

What's the difference between interim and major service?

Interim service includes oil change, filter replacement, and fluid checks. Major service adds air filter, cabin filter, spark plug, and transmission fluid service. Major service is more comprehensive and thorough.

Can I do van maintenance myself?

Some basic checks like oil level, tire pressure, and fluid levels can be done yourself. However, complex work like brake service, suspension work, and diagnostics should be handled by qualified technicians to ensure safety.

How do I know what items need service?

Your vehicle's owner's manual specifies service intervals and items. Service reminder lights or displays indicate when service is due. Professional mechanics can assess what needs attention.

What fluids should I check regularly?

Check engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering fluid, and windscreen washer fluid monthly. Transmission and differential fluids should be checked during servicing according to manufacturer recommendations.

How important is tire maintenance in the checklist?

Tire maintenance is critical. Check pressure monthly, inspect tread regularly, rotate tires every 5,000-7,500 miles, and replace tires when tread reaches 2mm. Proper tire maintenance ensures safety and extends tire life.

Why is brake inspection part of every service?

Brakes are essential for safety. Regular inspection catches wear early, preventing brake failure. During service, mechanics check pad thickness, rotor condition, brake fluid, and overall braking system functionality.
