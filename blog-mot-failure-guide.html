❓ MOT Failure Questions
What are the most common MOT failures?

Most common failures include brake problems (worn pads, poor stopping distance), lighting defects (broken bulbs, misalignment), tire issues (worn tread, damage), suspension wear, exhaust leaks, and rust on structural components.

How can I prevent MOT failure?

Regular maintenance is the best prevention. Check brakes, tires, lights, and fluids before MOT. Address warning signs immediately. Following manufacturer service schedules significantly reduces failure rates.

What is a major MOT failure vs minor?

Major failures (dangerous faults) cause immediate failure. Examples include brake failure, broken lights, bald tires. Minor failures (advisories) don't cause failure but should be addressed. Vehicle must pass major tests.

How much does MOT retest cost?

MOT retest typically costs £35-55. If original MOT passed, you have 10 days for free retest after repairs. Full retests after that period cost the standard MOT fee.

How long does MOT last?

MOT is valid for 12 months from the date issued. You should renew before expiry. When renewing, you can book appointment up to one month before expiry without invalidating your current MOT.

What should I check before MOT appointment?

Check tire tread and pressure, brake pad thickness, lighting system (all bulbs working), fluid levels, windscreen condition, mirrors, wipers, seatbelts, and listen for unusual engine noises.

Can I repair MOT failures myself?

Some basic repairs you can do yourself (replace bulbs, top up fluids). However, complex repairs like brakes, suspension, and exhaust should be handled by professionals to ensure safety compliance.

What is MOT advisory?

Advisories are items needing attention soon but don't cause test failure. Examples include worn wipers, minor corrosion, low tread tires not yet illegal. Address advisories to prevent future failures.
