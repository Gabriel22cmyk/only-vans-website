Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about van servicing and repairs

How much does a van service cost?

Van servicing costs vary depending on the make, model, and type of service required. A standard interim service typically starts from around £150, while a full service with all filters, fluids, and checks costs from around £250. We always provide a clear quote before starting any work — no hidden charges.

How often should I service my van?

Most manufacturers recommend servicing every 12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever comes first. If your van does high mileage or heavy commercial use, more frequent servicing (every 6 months) helps prevent breakdowns and keeps repair costs down. We follow manufacturer service schedules to maintain your warranty.

Do you service all van makes and models?

Yes — we service all makes and models of vans and commercial vehicles. Ford, Mercedes, Volkswagen, Renault, Peugeot, Citroën, Fiat, Vauxhall, Toyota — whatever you drive, we can service and repair it. We carry a wide range of parts and have access to manufacturer diagnostic software.

Can you do MOT testing for vans?

Yes, we offer MOT testing for vans and commercial vehicles up to Class 7 (3,500kg). Our DVSA-approved technicians carry out thorough testing to keep your vehicle road-legal. If any work is needed, we can carry out repairs on-site and retest — saving you time and hassle.

Do you offer breakdown recovery?

Yes — we offer breakdown recovery and vehicle transport services. If your van breaks down in the Oxfordshire area, call us and we’ll arrange collection. We can recover your vehicle back to our workshop for diagnosis and repair, getting you back on the road as quickly as possible.
