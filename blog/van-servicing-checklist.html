Van Servicing Checklist: What Should Be Included?

Everything a proper van service should cover — so you know what you're paying for and nothing gets missed.

Your van is your livelihood. If it breaks down, you don't work. Regular servicing is the most effective way to prevent breakdowns, reduce repair costs, and keep your van running reliably for years.

But what should actually be included in a van service? Here's the complete checklist we follow at Only Vans.

Interim Service (Every 6 Months or 6,000 Miles)

An interim service is a lighter check designed for high-mileage vans or those doing heavy commercial use. It covers the essentials:

  • Engine oil and filter change — fresh oil protects engine components and improves efficiency
  • Top up all fluids — coolant, brake fluid, power steering, screenwash
  • Tyre check — pressure, tread depth, condition, uneven wear patterns
  • Brake inspection — pad thickness, disc condition, brake fluid level
  • Battery check — voltage, terminals, corrosion
  • Lights and wipers — all bulbs working, wiper blade condition
  • Visual inspection — leaks, damage, exhaust condition

Full Service (Every 12 Months or 12,000 Miles)

A full service includes everything in an interim service, plus a deeper inspection:

  • All interim service items
  • Air filter replacement — a clogged air filter reduces performance and fuel economy
  • Fuel filter check/replacement — especially important on diesel vans
  • Cabin/pollen filter replacement — keeps the air clean inside the cab
  • Spark plugs (petrol engines) — worn plugs cause misfires and poor fuel economy
  • Drive belt inspection — check for cracks, wear, tension
  • Coolant system check — hoses, thermostat, radiator condition
  • Suspension check — shocks, springs, bushings, steering components
  • Exhaust system inspection — leaks, corrosion, mounting
  • Clutch check — bite point, wear, fluid level
  • Handbrake adjustment — travel and holding power
  • Door hinges and locks — lubrication and function
  • Diagnostic scan — read and clear any stored fault codes

How Much Does a Van Service Cost?

Van servicing costs depend on the make, model, and type of service:

  • Interim service: From around £150
  • Full service: From around £250
  • Major service (with timing belt/chain): £400–£900+

These prices are for standard commercial vans. Specialist work like wet belt replacement on Ford Transit EcoBlue engines is priced separately.

We always provide a clear quote before starting any work. No surprises, no hidden charges.

How Often Should You Service Your Van?

Follow the manufacturer's recommended schedule — typically:

  • Standard use: Every 12 months or 12,000 miles
  • Heavy commercial use: Every 6 months or 6,000 miles
  • High mileage (20,000+ per year): Consider interim services between full services

Regular servicing also maintains your warranty compliance and keeps your van's resale value higher.

What Happens If You Skip Services?

Skipping services might save you £250 today, but it costs you far more in the long run:

  • Worn oil causes increased engine wear, leading to premature failure
  • Clogged filters reduce performance and fuel economy — you're burning more diesel for less power
  • Worn brakes become a safety hazard and cost more to repair when discs need replacing too
  • Missed warning signs turn into major repairs — a £50 coolant hose becomes a £2,000 head gasket
  • MOT failures from issues that would have been caught during a service
