About Only Vans

Independent van specialists serving Wantage and Oxfordshire with expertise, honesty, and passion for vehicles.

Who We Are

Only Vans is an independent van specialist garage based in Lyford, Wantage, Oxfordshire. We've been serving the local community for years, building a reputation on honest advice, quality work, and genuine expertise.

We're not a corporate chain or a dealership — we're a small, tight-knit team of experienced technicians who care about doing the job right. We know our vans inside and out, and we know our customers by name.

Our mission: Keep your van on the road. Whether it's a full service, emergency repairs, or a wet belt replacement, we'll be honest with you about what needs doing and get you back to work as quickly as possible.
